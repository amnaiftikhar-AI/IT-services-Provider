IT SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

Section 1: Critical Incidents
Critical incidents require an initial response within 15 minutes and a resolution target of 120 minutes.

Section 2: High Incidents
High priority incidents require an initial response within 30 minutes and a resolution target of 240 minutes.

Section 3: Medium Incidents
Medium priority incidents require an initial response within 120 minutes and a resolution target of 480 minutes.

Section 4: Low Incidents
Low priority incidents require an initial response within 240 minutes and a resolution target of 1440 minutes.
